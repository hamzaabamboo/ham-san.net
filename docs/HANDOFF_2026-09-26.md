## 3. Environment and operational rules

- **Blender:**
  - Blender 5.2 with the official Blender Lab MCP on 127.0.0.1:9876.
  - Use only `mcp__blender__execute_blender_code`. Every call must `import bpy` and set `result` to a dict.
  - Never raise `SystemExit` and never `exec` a missing file. Either one killed Blender this session and lost unsaved edits.
- **Serialized save/export chain.** Run in this order and never overlap the steps:
  1. `bpy.ops.wm.save_as_mainfile(filepath=bpy.data.filepath)`
  2. `exec` `tools/room-harness/build/physics_audit.py`
  3. `exec` `tools/room-harness/build/audit_scene.py`
  4. `exec` `assets/room/export_room_web.py`. It runs synchronously in about 1–2 s; wrap it in `contextlib.redirect_stdout` to keep the output small.
  5. `node assets/room/strip_unused_uv.mjs assets/room/room-web-current.glb assets/room/room-web-lean.glb`
  6. `cp assets/room/room-web-lean.glb apps/astro/public/models/room.glb`
  7. `node tools/room-harness/build/check-build.mjs --json`
- **Render helper:** `tools/room-harness/build/render_views.py`. Call it via `exec` with `VIEWS=[(name,(x,y,z),(tx,ty,tz),lens,w,h)]`, `OUT_DIR` and `SAMPLES`. It creates temporary cameras and deletes them afterwards. Output goes to `tools/room-harness/evidence/build/work/v_<name>.png`.

## 5. Methodology and decision record

- **Approved art only.** The replacement art broke ART-01 and AST-11, and the owner rejected it explicitly. Improvements to art are limited to reframing UVs inside the approved atlases. New art needs owner approval first. Memory: `room-approved-art-only`.
- **Restore by UV, not by importing checkpoint meshes.** The checkpoint meshes carry the pre-remodel coordinates; the closet prints were about 1.2 m off. The live meshes were kept, and only the material and planar UVs were reassigned.
- **Clear-case fix lives in the runtime, not the GLB.** The Blender Cycles look needs transmission. On the web, a transmissive pane writes depth and samples a blurred, low-resolution buffer, which hid the prints behind it. So the web build downgrades only materials whose name starts with `room/acrylicclear case`.
- **Navigation anchors are data in the GLB.** `add_skill_toys.py` wrote them once, and they went stale when the props moved to the low table. For every target, verify that the nav position lies inside the tagged object bounds. The audit call in ledger row 13 is the check to reuse.
- **Hotspot tags must not sit on walls or floors.** A tag on a wall or floor turns ordinary clicks and taps into interactions.

Mobile CDP pattern:
1. Take the port from `get cdp-url`.
2. Call `GET http://127.0.0.1:<port>/json/list` and pick the page target.
3. Send `Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride` with `{width:390,height:844,deviceScaleFactor:2,mobile:true}`.
4. Send `Emulation.setTouchEmulationEnabled` with `{enabled:true,maxTouchPoints:5}`.
5. Navigate, then tap with `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` (`touchStart`/`touchEnd`).
6. Read state with `Runtime.evaluate` and capture with `Page.captureScreenshot`.
7. Keep the socket open for the whole test; the emulation ends when it closes.
