# A photo appears only when the post names someone

The photos page lists a post only when the post names at least one person: an `@handle`, a known name, or a performer hashtag. Posts without a name (tickets, match reports, notices, unnamed stage shots) are left out rather than labelled with a guessed name. Ham asked for proper portraits with names, and a wrong name on someone's photo is worse than a missing photo. To bring an unnamed post back, edit it on X to include the name. Do not add a manual override list.
