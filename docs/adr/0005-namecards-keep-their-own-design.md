# Namecard pages keep their own design

The namecard pages use `NamecardLayout`, with each card's own artwork, colours and typography, and none of the site shell, palette or brand mark. The cards are fan-community namecards handed out at events, and each design is a deliberate artwork. Restyling them to the Atelier system would erase what they are. Every other page follows `docs/design/README.md`.
