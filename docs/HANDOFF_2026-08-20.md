## 5. Methodology and decision record

**The method that worked, in execution order:**

1. **Freeze the tree, then audit.** Early rounds audited work done *in the same round* and found a flat 20–28 findings each time, mostly self-inflicted. Freezing the tree before an audit changed the character entirely — far more long-standing gaps, far fewer churn artifacts.
2. **Measure the rendered element, never the source.** Every significant defect this session was invisible in source and obvious in `getComputedStyle`. Examples: an invalid `background: var(--x), var(--x)` shorthand that the browser dropped entirely, leaving the featured card transparent; `data-leader={false}` rendering in Astro as the *attribute* `data-leader="false"`, so `[data-leader]` matched all 75 chart elements.
3. **Verify the whole element, not the property you changed.** Round 41 verified `textTransform` and `fontSize` matched and declared a fix done; the actual defect was geometry (207 px vs 66 px). **Measuring the property you changed is not measuring the element.**
4. **Fix the class, not the instance.** Three times a class was reported closed while an instance survived (§8 T3). The durable fix is a guard that scans *every* page for the pattern, not the files that were touched.
5. **Prove every guard red.** A guard is not accepted until the exact regression is reintroduced and the suite fails. Eight guards that passed by construction were found this session — including `expect(source).toContain('escapeXml')`, satisfied by the file's own `import` line.
6. **Adversarial verification with refutation as the default.** In round 39 every finding faced two verifiers — one instructed to refute, one judging whether fixing it improves the product. 18 of 38 findings died there. The synthesis noted this is itself evidence of taste: "Sites without taste do not generate that many false positives."
7. **Reject findings that do not reproduce.** Two `/th` findings (serif fallback, synthetic italic) were raised and refuted **three separate times** by measurement (h1 renders at 443 px = Noto Serif Thai; sans would be 416).
