## Implementation constraints extracted from the archive

- Keep only the main rectangular room in this slice; kitchen imagery is recorded as future reference and is not modeled.
- Treat layout evidence as authoritative for adjacency: PC desk, two window zones, dartboard/plush rack, display shelf/cases, penlight/towel grid, square floor table, beanbag, closet, door, and balcony.
- Use real-object cues for the next polish pass: short varied penlights, clear acrylic thickness and cut-to-silhouette bases, organized book/magazine/album lanes, varied lying plush forms, a square floor table, equipment-like PC/laptop stands, visible sockets/cables, and non-blocking poster groupings.
- Preserve interaction requirements: no-drag camera operation, WASD/checkpoint movement, lie-down mode that locks movement while looking toward the PC, closable closet, independent curtain states with the PC-side left curtain closed during daytime, readable HTML overlays, and existing light/day-night controls.
