# Room model design specification

Status: production reference contract. This document is not a completion claim and does not replace `conductor/room-spec.md` or `conductor/CURRENT_TASK.md`.

## Purpose

Build the owner's actual main room as a minimal, readable 3D scene. Minimal means reduced unrelated clutter while preserving the room's geometry, object categories, density, silhouette, material distinction, and browsing targets. The scene must remain recognizably the same room from the supplied photos and the latest floorplan.

The photorealistic v4 concept raster is not a model reference. The authoritative reference for geometry and placement is the latest floorplan; the authoritative reference for object shape, material, and density is the supplied room and shelf photography. The model is authored in Blender through the native Blender MCP, saved to `assets/room/room.blend`, exported to `apps/astro/public/models/room.glb`, and inspected in the headed browser.

## Global 3D language

- Silhouette first: each asset must read at thumbnail size before texture detail is added.
- Geometry is restrained but manufactured: bevels, seams, supports, folds, thickness, contact surfaces, and cast shadows are retained where they identify the object.
- No raw photo planes, blurred placeholders, repeated blank boxes, generic toy blocks, or photorealistic concept-board substitutions.
- Graphic surfaces use minimal illustrations with preserved silhouette, palette, and series differences. Never use a source photo with its original perspective and lighting pasted onto a plane.
- Materials are physically separated: painted wall, oak floor, white powder-coated metal, plywood, fabric, paper, frosted glass, clear acrylic, emissive plastic, and warm skin/plush textiles.
- Keep camera-facing detail on the visible side and simplify hidden backs. Every object still needs thickness and a supported base.
- Fixed layouts only. No random placement, seeded scattering, or density generated by repeated identical rectangles.

#### GEO-SHELL

- Source / current nodes: `014827641.PANO`, `015010154`, `015016170`; `Web Floor base`, `Web Staggered oak floor`, `Web Skirting`, `Web Right wall`.
- Geometry: model one rectangular floor slab, four wall planes with real thickness, ceiling slab, skirting, entry threshold and wall returns around every opening. Cut openings into the wall mesh; do not float a window plane in front of an uncut wall.
- Proportion / anchor: floorplan rectangle is the master; keep the two upper-wall openings, left PC wall, right shelf wall, lower-left entry and lower-right closet in those relative positions. Use guide empties for every wall corner.
- Finish / UV: oak planks run along the room length with staggered joints; warm-white paint is continuous; skirting has a small bevel and darker top shadow. No noise texture may erase the wall joins.
- State / target: no route target; `roomBounds` and `roomCollision` own the shell. Main room only; kitchen remains unmodelled reference.
- Proof / reject: top-down orthographic plus four interior corners must show a continuous ceiling/wall boundary. Reject any disconnected wall, missing lower return, hidden opening, or kitchen extrusion.

## Build order and gates

1. **Layout gate:** shell, both openings on one wall, darts axis, PC wall, shelf wall, display wall, entry, closet, table, beanbag, and two mat axes. Produce orthographic top view.
2. **Primary-form gate:** desk/T feet, chair curve, keyboard stand, darts stand/board/mat, shelf frame/boards, closet leaves, balcony edge. Produce full-room and four corner views.
3. **Collection gate:** teal bays, book families, folders, albums, cases, cut-to-silhouette stands, plush rack, plush variation. Produce shelf front/oblique/tight-bay views.
4. **Display-wall gate:** compact penlight grid, emissive tubes, long towels, uchiwa/flag. Produce daytime and warm-night views.
5. **Equipment gate:** monitor/laptop/keyboard/socket/cables/camera/Rubik/notes/namecard/table contents. Produce PC close-up and lie-down camera view.
6. **Material gate:** UV, normals, transparent cases, frosted/clear glass, cloth folds, wood grain, contact shadows. Compare same camera in Blender, GLB, and browser.
7. **Interaction gate:** targets, hints, pointer-lock/fallback, examine, events, hobbies, darts, curtain, switch, closet, lie-down. No geometry acceptance is implied by a working panel.
8. **Export gate:** save `.blend`, export GLB, verify hash/metadata/asset fetch, then inspect the same proof cameras in headed browser.

## Hard rejects

- photorealistic room board used in place of the 3D scene;
- sparse generic shelf, thick blank books, repeated rainbow blocks, or wide empty bays;
- cloudy acrylic, rectangular transparent plates, uncut character silhouettes, or unsupported bases;
- three long blank penlight rods, missing towel wall, or a clothes hanger in place of the display;
- wall-mounted darts without the independent stand/rack and straight mat;
- keyboard rotated away from playing position;
